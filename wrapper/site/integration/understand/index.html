Above and below the line

Visible evidence is not underground evidence

Public data describes what anyone can see. What decides a design lies below the pavement, and is asked for or tested, not inferred.

How evidence is labelled

Open measured

Published observations, such as land cover, tree points or LiDAR canopy.

Open modelled

Published model results, such as the heat analysis or sponge suitability.

Open derived

Computed by the team from open data, with the method named. For screening only.

Restricted measured · request required

Exists and is held for safety, such as utility lines or the sewer network.

Site investigation required

Nobody knows it until it is tested on site, such as permeability or root extent.

Assumption

A value chosen for an illustration, such as the strip width in the Street Lab.

Unknown

Not known, and not yet clear who could know it.

Who to ask next

Unknown to us is not unknown to the city

Much of what a design needs exists, but is held for good reasons: safety, privacy or professional responsibility. The right next step is a question to the right office.
