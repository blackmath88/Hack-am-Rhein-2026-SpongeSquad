# Who makes a sponge city in Basel

Team research note, 4 October 2026. It supports the website's DECIDE and UNDERSTAND pages and the pitch.

Status labels follow the [research method](../source-repo/docs/RESEARCH_METHOD.md): **VERIFIED** means checked against the linked Basel source during this pass; **INTERPRETATION** is the team's framing; **VERIFY** still needs a source.

## The idea

> A sponge-city intervention succeeds only when rain, roots, roads, pipes, soil and care agree.

No single map, model or office decides whether a street can hold rain. Each actor owns one question. The product makes those questions, and the evidence behind them, visible. It does not answer them on anyone's behalf. **INTERPRETATION**

## What Basel says

- The Stadtklimakonzept steers climate adaptation with thirteen measures and nine action fields. Depaving streets, planting trees and greening facades "sounds simple, but needs a lot of preparation and coordination." **VERIFIED** [Umsetzung Stadtklimakonzept](https://www.bs.ch/fr/node/30676)
- A Schwammstadt takes up rainwater like a sponge. Wherever possible, water is collected, retained, infiltrated, evaporated or used locally. It cools people, gives urban greenery better conditions and lowers flood risk. **VERIFIED** [same page](https://www.bs.ch/fr/node/30676)

## Stadtgärtnerei: keeper of the living system

> The Stadtgärtnerei tells Basel whether an intervention can become a healthy living system that grows, shades, cools and can be cared for over decades. Otherwise it is just a hole in the ground that receives rain.

- Its green-space maintenance department looks after all public parks and green spaces, street trees, cemeteries, school grounds, kindergartens and playgrounds. **VERIFIED** [Grünflächenunterhalt](https://www.bs.ch/bvd/stadtgaertnerei/unsere-abteilungen/gruenflaechenunterhalt)
- Since 2021 an interdepartmental working group led by the Bau- und Verkehrsdepartement (Stadtgärtnerei) has worked on Schwammstadt. It writes foundations, accompanies area developments and develops pilot projects, for example neighbourhood streets without a sewer connection in VoltaNord. **VERIFIED** [Grosser Rat report on the Stadtklimakonzept](https://grosserrat.bs.ch/dokumente/100405/000000405284.pdf)
- Pilot at Riehenring, 2022: 14 parking bays rebuilt with permeable grass-joint stones over different substrates. **VERIFIED** [same report](https://grosserrat.bs.ch/dokumente/100405/000000405284.pdf)
- Unsealing and planting projects led by the Stadtgärtnerei include Triangel-Platz (2021, 1,500 m² unsealed, 18 new trees) and the Elsässerrheinweg (2023–2024). **VERIFIED** [same report](https://grosserrat.bs.ch/dokumente/100405/000000405284.pdf)
- Climate-adapted care: restrained pruning to maximise canopy shade, heat-resistant tree species. **VERIFIED** [same report](https://grosserrat.bs.ch/dokumente/100405/000000405284.pdf)
- Multifunctional tree-site substrates and tree-integrated retention or infiltration basins as Stadtgärtnerei work streams. **VERIFY**: not found in the sources read. The team catalogue lists a ZHAW project on substrates and vegetation for three Basel streets.
- That the Stadtgärtnerei maintains the tree cadastre. **VERIFY**: the cadastre is published on data.bs.ch; its owning unit was not checked.

What its question adds to a design (**INTERPRETATION**):

| It asks | Why it changes a design |
| --- | --- |
| Can water reach roots without drowning them? | A tree trench needs a wet and dry rhythm, not permanent waterlogging. |
| Is there enough rootable, aerated soil? | Trees need space and air under a street that still carries loads. |
| Can the planting survive heat, drought, salt, sediment and short flooding? | Many rain-garden concepts fail because the plants were not chosen for street stress. |
| Can it be cleaned, weeded, pruned, repaired and funded? | A feature nobody can maintain becomes a liability. |
| Does it protect the mature trees already there? | A new basin can damage the tree that provides the most cooling today. |

## Permissions for rainwater

- Infiltration, or discharge into a surface water, needs a permit from the AUE. Discharge into a public clean-water sewer also needs the Tiefbauamt's permit. The permit is usually granted within a building or sewer application. **VERIFIED** [AUE: Direkteinleitung von Regenwasser](https://www.bs.ch/wsu/aue/abteilung-gewaesser-und-boden/direkteinleitung-von-regenwasser)
- Within the AUE, the groundwater unit handles infiltration and the direct-discharge unit handles discharge to surface waters. **VERIFIED** [same page](https://www.bs.ch/wsu/aue/abteilung-gewaesser-und-boden/direkteinleitung-von-regenwasser)
- The same page says the general drainage plan (GEP) shows which areas suit infiltration. **VERIFIED** [same page](https://www.bs.ch/wsu/aue/abteilung-gewaesser-und-boden/direkteinleitung-von-regenwasser). Whether that GEP layer is openly available was not checked. **VERIFY**

## Utilities

- The Leitungskataster documents all underground supply and disposal lines in public space. It is an overview plan, different from an operator's works plan: it shows only position, type and size of lines and their structures. **VERIFIED** [Leitungskataster](https://www.bs.ch/bvd/grundbuch-und-vermessungsamt/leitungskataster)
- Depth, clearance and excavation approval therefore stay with the operators and the construction process. **INTERPRETATION** of the above.

## Coordinated street works

- The canton, BVB and IWB renew Basel's infrastructure regularly; about 100 to 120 street building sites are needed each year. **VERIFIED** [Tiefbauamt: Baustellen](https://www.bs.ch/bvd/tiefbauamt/baustellen)
- Operators report streets, tracks or lines that will soon need work. Together they set time and scope so that tracks, lines and street can be renewed at the same time. **VERIFIED** [same page](https://www.bs.ch/bvd/tiefbauamt/baustellen)
- The interactive map at baustellen.bs.ch shows current and upcoming projects, usually once a building permit exists. **VERIFIED** [same page](https://www.bs.ch/bvd/tiefbauamt/baustellen)
- So the best sponge-city opportunity is often the street that is about to be opened anyway, where trees, rainwater and public space can be redesigned with the works. The public map shows this late, after the permit. **INTERPRETATION**

## Guardrails

- The public map identifies opportunities. Controlled records identify constraints. Fieldwork verifies reality.
- No known public constraint is not the same as no constraint.
- Visible evidence is not underground evidence.
- A tree point is not a root-zone map.
- Modelled suitability is not construction feasibility.
- The product is a better first conversation, not a permit.
