1 · Who must agree

A sponge city is a team sport

A sponge-city intervention succeeds only when rain, roots, roads, pipes, soil and care agree.

Basel's own wording: depaving streets and planting trees "sounds simple, but needs a lot of preparation and coordination" (Umsetzung Stadtklimakonzept). This coordination protects what nobody can see from the street.

The keeper of the living system

Can it live here?

Stadtgärtnerei Basel-Stadt

The Stadtgärtnerei tells Basel whether an intervention can become a healthy living system that grows, shades, cools and can be cared for over decades. Otherwise it is just a hole in the ground that receives rain.

What it brings

  • It looks after all public parks and green spaces, street trees, school grounds, kindergartens and playgrounds. Source
  • Since 2021 it has led the canton's interdepartmental Schwammstadt working group: foundations, area developments and pilots such as VoltaNord streets without a sewer connection. Source
  • It tests real solutions: 14 parking bays at Riehenring rebuilt in 2022 with permeable grass-joint stones over different substrates. Source
  • It cares for shade: restrained pruning to maximise canopy, heat-resistant species. Source

Sources verified on 4 October 2026. Research note

Why its question changes a design

Roots and water
A tree trench needs a wet and dry rhythm, not permanent waterlogging.
Soil volume
Trees need rootable, aerated soil under a street that still carries loads.
Street stress
Planting must survive heat, drought, salt, sediment and short flooding.
Existing trees
A new basin must not damage the mature tree that cools the street today.
Care
A feature nobody can clean, prune, repair and fund is not a success.

3 · Investigations and approvals

What must be established before a proposal advances

Only rules found in Basel sources are stated as rules. Guidance from Bern, Zürich and Luzern is useful background but is not a Basel requirement.

Established for Basel

Permits, rules and practice found

  • Rainwater permits. Infiltration, or discharge into a surface water, needs an AUE permit. Discharge into a public clean-water sewer also needs the Tiefbauamt's. Usually granted within a building or sewer application. Source
  • Utility overview. The Leitungskataster shows position, type and size of lines in public space. It is not a works plan. Source
  • Joint street works. Canton, BVB and IWB set time and scope together so street, tracks and lines are renewed at once. Source
  • Green roofs. § 72 BPG: flat roofs of 10 m² or more must be greened when built or renovated. Source
  • Rainwater fee. CHF 0.90 per m² of sealed area connected to the sewer. Source
  • Pilots under monitoring. Sponge-city streets in VoltaNord, with five years of scientific monitoring. Source

Where opportunity really lies

The street that is about to be opened anyway

The best sponge-city opportunity is often not the wettest or hottest street. It is the street where utility renewal and street reconstruction are already planned, so trees, rainwater and public space can be redesigned with the works. The public construction map shows projects usually once a permit exists, which is late for design. The joint works coordination knows earlier. Source · Conclusion is team interpretation.

Next steps for a candidate street

A working checklist from the team, ordered by who to ask. It is not a documented Basel procedure.

  1. Fix a footprint and check its parcels and public-realm status.
  2. Map existing trees and protected root zones with the Stadtgärtnerei.
  3. Request the Leitungskataster overview, then confirm with IWB and other operators before any excavation.
  4. Clarify with the Tiefbauamt where retained and overflow water can go.
  5. Test soil and permeability on site, then ask the AUE about infiltration.
  6. Check the joint works coordination for a chance to build with planned works.

Team prototype

Rain Walk: residents as evidence collectors

A demo campaign in which people record what they see on a street, such as paving, gullies or tree pits, and review each other's notes. It is a prototype on a synthetic segment, not an official participation channel. A visual observation never establishes what is underground. Open Rain Walk
